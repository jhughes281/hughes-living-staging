Staged with furniture a buyer can take home

How it works

From an empty house to a sold sofa

  1. 1

    Add the house

    Create an account, then enter the address and the dates you know: when the furniture goes in, when it comes out.

  2. 2

    Choose the furniture

    Pick from what is free in our warehouse and place it room by room. The page tells you what a room is still missing.

  3. 3

    Print the QR card and tags

    One card for the kitchen counter, one tag for every piece. Mark the house staged and the buyer page opens.

  4. 4

    Buyers scan and buy

    They see every piece with its price and order from their phone. Hughes Living follows up, takes the payment and collects the piece.

At a showing

The buyer’s side is a phone and a price tag

No app and no account. A buyer points a phone camera at the card or at a tag and gets that home’s page: each room, each piece, its price, and whether it is still available.

  • Buy this piece. One piece, straight from its tag.
  • Buy the look. Everything still available in the house, priced as one order.
  • Ask a question. About a piece or the whole house.

An order is a request to Hughes Living, not a card payment on the page. We contact the buyer to confirm it.

When a piece sells

% of the sale comes off your staging fee

Hughes Living sets a staging fee for each house. Every piece that sells out of a house you are staging earns a credit against that fee, worked out on the price it sold for.

It is a credit on the fee, not a payment, and it cannot take the fee below $0. Your running total is on your dashboard.
